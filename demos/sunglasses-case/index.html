01 / The Philosophy

Most accessories are built to be replaced. We built one to be kept.

We took the cues from mid-century automotive design: thick hide, no logos, nothing to prove. The leather takes on the story of where you’ve been and darkens as it goes.

It’s heavy in the hand and quiet in your pocket. No fuss, no rattle, no gimmicks.

02 / Material Specification

Craft & materials

Spec sheet / Rev. 03
The Shell

2.0mm Full-Grain Cowhide

Vegetable-tanned at a historic tannery. Holds its shape, doesn’t crack, and ages into its own patina rather than wearing out.

The Core

Reinforced Internal Spine

A rigid, lightweight inner shell. Chuck it in a packed bag, drop it on tarmac, throw it in the glovebox. Your frames will be fine.

The Interior

Anti-Scratch Microfibre Lining

High-density Japanese microfibre suede. No lens abrasions, no damaged coatings.

The Hardware

Solid Antique Brass

A heavy snap closure with a proper click, not a flimsy one. Fittings are recessed, so no metal touches the lens.
